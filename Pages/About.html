About Our Project

Student Attendance Manager is a web-based project designed to simplify the process of managing student attendance digitally. It helps teachers maintain attendance records and allows students to view their attendance information easily.

Our Goal

To replace traditional paper-based attendance systems with a simple, organized and user-friendly digital solution.

Main Features

Student management, attendance tracking, reports generation, and performance monitoring.

Technology Used

HTML, CSS and JavaScript are used to create this responsive attendance management interface.
